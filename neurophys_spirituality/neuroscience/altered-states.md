# Altered States

*Neuroscience page — part of the [Neuroscience](index.md) series.*

## The short version

Meditation, psychedelics, near-death experiences, and even ordinary anesthesia all produce dramatic shifts in subjective experience that are real, reproducible, and increasingly well-studied mechanistically — without requiring anything supernatural to explain them. That doesn't make them any less profound; if anything, understanding the mechanism makes the phenomena more remarkable, not less.

## To fill in

- Meditation: documented effects on attention, default mode network activity, and long-term structural changes with extensive practice — separated clearly from unsupported claims (e.g. specific "energy" or "chakra" mechanisms)
- Psychedelics: default mode network disruption, ego dissolution, and the phenomenological overlap with NDE reports
- Near-death experiences: surges of coordinated brain activity at cardiac arrest, hypoxia, REM intrusion — mechanistic candidates, and what remains unexplained
- Terminal lucidity: real, documented, mechanistically unexplained — an honest "we don't know yet," not evidence for anything specific
- Anesthesia: we don't fully understand how most anesthetics produce unconsciousness either — a good humility check on how much is still unknown even in "boring," non-mystical contexts
