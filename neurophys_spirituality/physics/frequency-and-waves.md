# Frequency & Waves

*Physics concept page — part of the [Physics](index.md) series.*

## The short version

Frequency is a precise, measurable property of anything that oscillates: the number of cycles per second (measured in Hertz). It always describes a *specific physical thing that is oscillating* — a sound wave, a light wave, an electrical signal. It is never a free-floating property of a person, an object, or an intention on its own.
