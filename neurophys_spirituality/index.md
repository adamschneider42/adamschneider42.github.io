# Physics, Neuroscience, and the Edge of Mystery

I've always been drawn to mysticism and fringe physics — the questions at the edge of what we understand are genuinely fascinating. But there's a difference between sitting with a real mystery and being sold a fake answer dressed up in scientific language.

This site is my own reference for sorting the two apart: what physics and neuroscience actually say, what's still genuinely open and interesting, and where the language of "energy," "frequency," and "quantum" gets borrowed and abused to sell something that isn't there.

## Two ways to browse

**[By physics concept](/physics/)** — the actual science, building from classical mechanics up through quantum field theory. Read in order if you want the full picture.

**[By claim](/claims/)** — start here if you came in wondering about something specific (manifestation, quantum consciousness, water memory, near-death experiences...). Each entry points back to the physics or neuroscience that actually addresses it.

Also: **[Neuroscience & the frontier](/neuroscience/)** — consciousness research, altered states, and what's actually new and interesting in the literature right now.
