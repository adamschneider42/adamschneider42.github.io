# Consciousness Basics

*Neuroscience page — part of the [Neuroscience](index.md) series.*

## The short version

Neuroscience has made real, substantial progress on *correlates* of consciousness (what brain activity accompanies which conscious experiences) without solving the deeper "hard problem" — why there is subjective experience at all, rather than just information processing in the dark. That gap between "solid empirical progress" and "still an open mystery" is exactly the space where spiritual claims tend to insert unfounded certainty in either direction.
