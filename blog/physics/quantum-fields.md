# Quantum Fields

*Physics concept page — part of the [Physics](index.md) series.*

## The short version

Quantum field theory (QFT) combines quantum mechanics with fields: instead of particles being tiny billiard balls, they're excitations ("ripples") in an underlying field that fills all of space. This is genuinely one of the strangest and most successful frameworks in physics — and also one of the most abused in spiritual marketing, since "quantum field" sounds impressive and is hard to fact-check without a physics background.
