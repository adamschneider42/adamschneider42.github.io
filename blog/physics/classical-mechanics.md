# Classical Mechanics

*Physics concept page — part of the [Physics](index.md) series.*

## The short version

Classical mechanics describes how objects move under forces — and defines precisely what physicists mean by "energy," "force," and "momentum." These words have exact, quantitative meanings that are almost never what wellness marketing means when it borrows them.
