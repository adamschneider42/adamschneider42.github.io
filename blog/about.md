# About this project

I have a PhD in computational neuroscience and work in ML applied to remote sensing. I've also always been drawn to mysticism and fringe physics — genuinely, not as a punching bag. But I don't want to confuse "interesting and unresolved" with "true because it would be cool."

This site is where I keep that sorting process organized: what physics and neuroscience actually show, what's genuinely still open and worth following, and where the language of "energy," "quantum," and "frequency" gets borrowed by spiritual and wellness marketing to sell something that isn't really there.

It started as a personal reference. Some of it is turning into short videos. Feedback and comments get folded back into revisions here.
